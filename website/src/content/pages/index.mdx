  <Feature icon="02" title="Per-source control">

  </Feature>
  <Feature icon="04" title="GPU compositing">
    A wgpu pipeline with minimal CPU to GPU copies, so the frame rate holds as the grid grows.
  </Feature>
  <Feature icon="05" title="Multi-protocol output">
    Send the composed view out over NDI, DeckLink, Syphon or Spout, or keep it as a clean canvas.
  </Feature>
  <Feature icon="06" title="Native everywhere">

  </Feature>
