## Platform requirements

- **macOS**: Apple Silicon (arm64), macOS 12.3 or later.
- **Windows**: Windows 10 or later, 64-bit.
- **Linux**: the `.deb` covers Debian and Ubuntu; the AppImage runs on any modern glibc distribution.

## First run

1. Open the app and follow your system's prompt
2. Add a source from the side panel and start arranging it on the canvas.
3. Add an output when you are ready to send the composed view somewhere.

NDI support uses the runtime bundled with the download.
