## Moving and resizing

Drag a source body to move it, and drag a corner or edge handle to resize. Hold the modifier key to preserve the aspect
ratio while resizing.

## Snapping

While dragging, sources snap to the canvas edges, the canvas centre, and the edges of other sources. Guides appear when
a snap is available. Snapping keeps layouts tidy without pixel-hunting.

## Zoom and pan

- Scroll to zoom around the pointer.
- Drag the background to pan when zoomed in.
- Use the **View** menu to fit the canvas to the window or reset the zoom.

## Borders and labels

Each source can show a border and a label. Borders make adjacent feeds readable, and labels name the source on screen.
Both are toggled per source in its settings.

## Flips

Horizontal and vertical flips are available per source, for mirroring a camera or correcting an inverted feed.
