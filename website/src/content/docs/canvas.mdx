## Moving and resizing

Drag a source body to move it, and drag a corner or edge handle to resize. Hold the modifier key to preserve the aspect
ratio while resizing.

## Snapping

While dragging, sources snap to the canvas edges, the canvas centre, and the edges of other sources. Guides appear when
a snap is available. Snapping keeps layouts tidy without pixel-hunting.

## Zoom and pan

- Scroll to zoom around the pointer.
- Drag the background to pan when zoomed in.
- Use the **View** menu to fit the canvas to the window or reset the zoom.

## Expanding a source

Expand a source to look at it on its own, filling the whole canvas: select it and press `F`, use **Edit > Expand Source**,
or right-click it and choose **Expand to full canvas**. The outputs change with it, so a receiver sees only that source
while it stays expanded.

Press `Esc`, click the close button at the top of the canvas, or use **Edit > Exit Expanded View** to return to the full
layout. While a source is expanded the canvas only pans; sources cannot be moved or resized.

## Borders and labels

Each source can show a border and a label. Borders make adjacent feeds readable, and labels name the source on screen.
Both are toggled per source in its settings.

## Global settings

The canvas size and the default label and border for every source are set once in **Global Settings** in the side panel.
