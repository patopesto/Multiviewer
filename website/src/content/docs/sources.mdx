## Adding a source

Choose a protocol in the side panel, then pick a discovered source. NDI and Syphon sources are discovered continuously;
capture devices refresh on demand.

## Reconnecting

If a source disappears, Multiviewer keeps it on the canvas and reconnects when it returns. Its position and label are
preserved.

## GPU sources

Syphon and Spout deliver a texture straight to the compositor, with no CPU copy. The other protocols deliver frames that
the compositor uploads to the GPU once per frame.
