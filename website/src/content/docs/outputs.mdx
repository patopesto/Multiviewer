## Adding an output

Open **Outputs** in the side panel, pick a protocol, and configure it. Enabled outputs receive the composed canvas
every frame.

<ProtocolMatrix protocols={protocols} direction="output" columns={['platforms', 'notes']} />

## Frame rate and backpressure

Outputs run at the application's frame rate, while each consumer reads at its own rate. When a destination is busy,
Multiviewer skips a frame for that output rather than stalling the canvas, so a slow consumer does not affect the others
or the compositor.

## Syphon and Spout

These outputs share the canvas as a GPU texture with no readback, which keeps latency low for other applications on the
same machine. They are only active while a consumer is connected.

## Protocol settings

Only DeckLink has per-output settings; the others are enabled and disabled from the **Outputs** panel.

### DeckLink

The Blackmagic Desktop Video driver need to be installed on your machine. Download it from [Blackmagic's website](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/support/family/capture-and-playback)

Choose the playback device and the video mode sent to it. The frame rate follows the selected mode.
